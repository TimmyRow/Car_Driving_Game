# Asset provenance

All car geometry, road geometry, architecture, vegetation, signage, environment layout, and procedural effects are original code-authored assets for Velocity Coast. There are no licensed car brands or Asphalt assets in the game.

## Photographic materials

The following materials are by Poly Haven and are used under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). They are bundled locally, so playing the game does not contact Poly Haven.

| Material | Source | Bundled files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rock 3 | [Poly Haven Rock 3](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_3) | `public/textures/rock_3_diff_1k.webp`, `public/textures/rock_3_nor_gl_1k.webp` |
| Asphalt 02 | [Poly Haven Asphalt 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02) | `public/textures/asphalt_02_diff_1k.webp`, `public/textures/asphalt_02_nor_gl_1k.webp` |

Source images were the original 1K JPG diffuse and OpenGL normal maps downloaded from `https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/`. Diffuse maps retain 1024×1024 resolution; normal maps are reduced to 512×512. All four are encoded to WebP at quality 85 for browser delivery. Combined file size: **982,358 bytes** (about 959 KiB). The `_1k` filename suffix identifies the source set; normal-map output resolution is 512×512.

The photographic maps provide stone/asphalt surface detail. Mesh colors provide original regional tinting, and all race markings are separate geometry for clear visibility.
